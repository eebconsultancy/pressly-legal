Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pressly handles information when you use the Pressly iOS application. Pressly is provided by EEB Consultancy LLC.

1. Overview

Pressly is designed to help you record, organize, review, and export health-related information such as blood pressure, pulse, blood oxygen, and blood glucose readings.

We aim to minimize the collection of personal information and use device-level features where practical.

2. Health Information

Depending on the features you use, Pressly may process health-related information including:

  • Systolic and diastolic blood pressure readings
  • Pulse and heart-rate readings
  • Blood oxygen readings that you enter
  • Blood glucose readings that you enter
  • Dates and times associated with your readings
  • Information imported from or exported to Apple Health when you authorize it

Blood pressure, blood oxygen, and blood glucose values are not measured by the iPhone itself. These values must come from information you enter or from supported health-data sources that you authorize.

3. Camera-Based Heart Rate

Pressly may use the iPhone camera and flash to estimate your heart rate by analyzing changes in the camera signal while your fingertip is placed over the camera and flash.

Camera access is used for this heart-rate feature only when you choose to use it and grant camera permission.

Pressly does not use the camera to measure blood pressure, blood oxygen, or blood glucose.

4. Camera Images and Video

The camera-based heart-rate feature analyzes camera frames as part of the measurement process. Pressly is not intended to create a photo or video recording of your fingertip as part of this feature.

Camera access remains subject to the permissions and controls provided by iOS.

5. Apple Health

Pressly may integrate with Apple Health through HealthKit. HealthKit access is optional and requires your explicit authorization.

Depending on the permissions you choose to grant, Pressly may read or write supported health information for features available in the app. You can control Health permissions through Apple's Health and Settings applications.

Pressly does not use HealthKit data for advertising and does not sell HealthKit data.

6. Local Data Storage

Health records and related app information may be stored locally on your device so that Pressly can provide history, trends, insights, reports, and other app functionality.

Information stored locally is subject to the security and backup behavior of your device and the services you choose to use.

7. Reports and Exports

Pressly allows you to create or export information, including reports and data files such as PDF or CSV files.

When you choose to share or save an exported file outside Pressly, that file may be handled by Apple or by the application, service, or destination you select. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

You are responsible for deciding where you send or store exported health information.

8. Notifications and Reminders

If you enable reminders, Pressly may request permission to send local notifications. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.

Reminder information is used to provide the reminder functionality you configure.

9. Information We Do Not Sell

We do not sell your health information or personal information.

Health information accessed through HealthKit is not used for advertising, marketing, or data-broker purposes.

10. Purchases

Pressly may offer subscriptions, introductory offers, or one-time purchases through Apple's in-app purchase system.

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your full payment-card information. Purchase and entitlement information may be processed as necessary to determine access to premium features.

11. Third-Party Services

Pressly may rely on Apple platform services required to provide app functionality, including services such as the App Store, HealthKit, system notifications, and system sharing features.

When information is handled by Apple or another service you explicitly choose to interact with, that service's own privacy terms may also apply.

12. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored locally by Pressly generally remains on your device until you delete individual records, use an available data-deletion feature, or remove the application, subject to device backups and information stored separately in services such as Apple Health.

Deleting information from Pressly does not necessarily delete information previously exported, shared, backed up, or independently stored in Apple Health.

13. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the application to protect information handled by Pressly. However, no device, storage system, or method of electronic processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Children's Privacy

Pressly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

15. Medical Disclaimer

Pressly is intended for informational and personal tracking purposes. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical care.

Do not delay or disregard professional medical advice because of information displayed by Pressly. If you believe you may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or an appropriate healthcare professional.

16. International Users

Pressly may be available in multiple countries. Privacy and data protection rights may vary depending on where you live.

You may contact us if you have questions regarding your personal information or applicable privacy rights.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Pressly, applicable laws, or our practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
